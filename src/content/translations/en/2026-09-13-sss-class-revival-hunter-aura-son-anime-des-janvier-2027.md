It's now official: **SSS-Class Revival Hunter is getting an anime**.

The adaptation was announced in September 2026 with a first trailer and a visual. Its broadcast will begin in **January 2027**, and Crunchyroll has confirmed that the series will be available in many regions worldwide, including Europe.

For those who don't yet know the work, I'll deliberately stay very vague: *SSS-Class Revival Hunter* originated as a **Korean web novel**, later adapted into a webtoon. It features a modern fantasy universe built around hunters, powers, and a mysterious tower.

No need to know more before discovering the series.

## First Team Already Announced

The anime will be directed by **Hiroaki Sakurai**, with **Sayuri Oba** on script and **Takahiro Yoshimatsu** on character design. Animation is handled by **STUDIO fu**.

Two main Japanese voice actors have also been revealed: **Takeo Otsuka** and **Satoshi Hino**.

For now, no precise January date nor any episode count has been announced.

The announcement comes especially at a time when adaptations of Korean works are taking an increasingly visible place in Japanese animation. After the massive success of certain licenses originating from webtoons, *SSS-Class Revival Hunter* is among the next adaptations to watch.

The good news is we won't have to wait very long: **January 2027 is just a few months away**.
