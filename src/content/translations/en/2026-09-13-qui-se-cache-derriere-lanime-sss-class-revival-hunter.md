The announcement of a new anime is often accompanied by the same information: a date, a teaser, a few names and a studio.

The problem is that a list of names says almost nothing to someone who doesn’t spend his days reading Japanese generics.

for **SSS-Class Revival Hunter**Many important positions are already known.

Some names deserve to stop.

This article does not contain **No spoiler on the story**and We are talking only about the manufacture of the anime and officially announced information.

## STUDIO FU takes orders

The animation of *SSS-Class Revival Hunter* It is entrusted to **The studio fu**.

This is probably the least immediately identifiable name of the announcement, especially because it does not yet possess the international reputation of studios such as MAPPA, Madhouse, Bones or ufotable.

Studio Fu is a Japanese animation studio based in Musashino, Tokyo. The official website shows, in particular, participations in various recent productions, but *SSS-Class Revival Hunter* It represents a interesting production to follow because the studio is here explicitly credited for the animation of the series. 

However, a very common mistake should be avoided when talking about anime:

**The name of the studio does not allow, alone, to predict the final quality.**

An animated series depends greatly on the director, animation directors, recruited independent animators, calendar, pre-production and how the episodes are distributed between the different teams.

A large studio can produce a technically irregular series.

A less-known studio can produce a great surprise.

for *SSS-Class Revival Hunter*The rest of the team is important.

## Hiroaki Sakurai to the performance

The announced director is **by Hiroaki Sakurai**.

Its name is associated with *The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.*A series in which he also served as director. Crunchyroll also quotes it directly as a reference in the announcement of *SSS-Class Revival Hunter*. 

The role of an anime director is much broader than simply "to tell the animators what to draw."

He participates in the general direction of adaptation: rhythm, tone, stage, visual choices, coordination with the leaders of the different departments and coherence of the whole.

In an adaptation, a great part of the difficulty also consists in transforming a thought work to be read into something thought to be looked at.

A spectacular vertical image in a webtoon does not automatically have the same impact on a 16:9 screen.

A succession of boxes may have to become a scene of thirty seconds.

A silence can be extended.

One action can be completely re-thought.

This is where the work of achievement becomes decisive.

## A character designer who doesn’t come anywhere.

The name that probably attracted most my attention in this first team is **by Takahiro Yoshimatsu**Responsible for character design.

If you look a lot of anime, you may have already seen his work without knowing his name.

Yoshimatsu has worked as a character designer on the anime. **Hunter × Hunter 2011**In addition to several products related to **Overlord**and The official Japanese base JFDB credit for example to character design of resuming films*Overlord* The character design of the films *The Hunter*. 

Their role on *SSS-Class Revival Hunter* It will be fundamental.

Graphically adjusting a webtoon is not to reproduce each drawing to the same.

A character intended for animation must be able to be drawn again and again, in different angles, by many people, sometimes in extremely fast scenes.

The character designer is looking for a balance:

```text

identité visuelle de l'œuvre originale

              +

cohérence des personnages

              +

possibilité de les animer efficacement

```

An incredibly detailed design can be magnificent on a fixed illustration and become a nightmare when you need to produce several thousand drawings.

The quality of an adaptation is sometimes played in this kind of invisible compromise.

## Scenario will be supervised by Sayuri Oba

**by Sayuri Oba** It was announced in the script.

Here again, it deserves more than a line in a technical file.

When a webtoon becomes an anime, someone has to decide how to organize the material available in episodes of about twenty minutes.

Where to end an episode?

Which scenes have to breathe more?

What transitions work on a page but less well in animation?

How much content can be adjusted without giving the impression that the story runs after its own generic end?

These are decisions that can completely change the perception of an adaptation, even when the events remain identical to the original work.

For now, no sufficiently detailed official information allows to know the exact pace chosen by production.

And that’s probably better: announcing a team doesn’t yet mean we can guess exactly how the series will be structured.

## Two major votes are already known.

The first casting revealed includes:

**by Takeo Otsuka**He will play Kim Gongja.

**by Satoshi Hino**He will play Sword Emperor.

These two roles have been confirmed by both the official Japanese website and Crunchyroll. 

I will not voluntarily go further on the characters.

Even official presentations already reveal some starting elements that someone who wants to discover the anime completely blind might prefer to ignore.

For now, knowing who gives his voice to the two characters highlighted is largely enough.

## The first teaser does not yet allow to judge the animation

A teaser and a first video were published during the announcement.

They are important because they finally give an audiovisual identity to the project, but some care must be kept.

A few seconds don’t let you know:

- the regularity of the animation over a whole season;

- the quality of the most ambitious episodes;

- the final finish level;

- management of combat;

- the stability of the drawings;

- The real pace of adaptation.

A teaser first serves to present a production.

This is not a benchmark.

The real material that allows you to start seriously judging the adaptation will probably come with longer ad bands and, most importantly, when we get to know more team members.

## What we really know in September 2026

For now, some information is relatively simple.

The anime is intended for **January 2027**.

Hiroaki Sakurai will make it at home. **The studio fu**.

Sayuri Oba takes care of the script.

Takahiro Yoshimatsu realizes the character design.

Takeo Otsuka and Satoshi Hino are part of the main casting announced.

and **Crunchyroll will broadcast the series in Europe**This confirms its official availability for our region. 

However, at the time I write these lines, several important information has not yet been officially announced: **the exact date of broadcast, the number of episodes or the details of a possible French version**.

It is better to leave these boxes empty than to fill them with rumors.

## Now we have to see what this team will do.

A expected adaptation always comes accompanied by a strange reflection: we seek to immediately decide whether it will be excellent or failed while sometimes we have only one poster and thirty seconds of video.

for *SSS-Class Revival Hunter*It is still too early.

But we already have something more useful than a simple date: the first people who will have to turn a Korean webtoon into a Japanese animation series.

And among them, the presence of Takahiro Yoshimatsu in character design and Hiroaki Sakurai in realization gives at least real reasons to follow the production closely.
